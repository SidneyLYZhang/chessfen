## 3. Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space (X)** = 8.00 units on a 100-unit canvas (= 8.0% of the mark width). Keep this area free of any other element.
- **Minimum size**: 16 px (screen) / 12 mm (print). Below this, use the simplified variant.
- **Safe zone diagram**: `safe-zone.svg`

## 6. Misuse (prohibited)

- Do not recolor the mark with colors outside the specified palette.
- Do not stretch, condense, rotate, or apply perspective distortion.
- Do not add gradients, drop shadows, glows, or outlines.
- Do not place the mark on a busy background without the reversed variant or a solid panel behind it.
- Do not reduce below the minimum size or inside the clear space.
- Do not reassemble the primitives into a new composition.

## 8. Notes

Board + encoding. A 2x2 alternating checkerboard (top-left / bottom-right filled) is the minimal legible chessboard unit; three discrete diagonal strokes along the empty counter-diagonal stand for FEN rank separators (/) and PGN ordered move tokens. Plane (board) and line (code) interlock on one diagonal axis: the board compressed into text.

## 9. Motif semantics — 棋盘 / FEN / PGN

| Element | Geometry | Meaning |
|---------|----------|---------|
| 棋盘 | 2 个 38×38 实心方格，位于左上与右下，构成 2×2 交替格 | 8×8 棋盘的最小可识别抽象，深浅格交替 |
| FEN | 沿空白对角（左下→右上）等距排列的 3 段 `/` 方向短划 | FEN 用 `/` 分隔 8 个 rank，用 `8` 等数字压缩连续空格；离散段即被压缩的字符流 |
| PGN | 同一条对角上的等距分段 | `1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 …` 的按序着法流，每一段代表一步 |
| 关系 | 面（方格）与线（短划）分占棋盘的两条对角，互不重叠 | 把二维棋盘压缩成一维编码文本 |

- 两个实心格占「左上—右下」对角，三段短划走「右上—左下」空白对角，几何互补，因此 16 px 与单色下两组图元仍能分离。
- 不使用棋子字形、字母或数字字面量；编码特性完全由「离散、等距、线性」的节奏表达。
- 单色版中面与线同为 `#3B2A17`，靠**形状**（块 vs 划）而非颜色区分层级，故单色诊断为 PASS。

## 10. Favicon usage

- Light browser chrome: `favicon.svg` (modern browsers) with `favicon.ico` as the ICO fallback (16/32/48/64 px in one file).
- Dark browser chrome: **must** use `favicon-dark.ico`. In the full-color mark the three structural code strokes are `#3B2A17` and disappear on a dark tab, leaving only two gold squares — the encoding semantics would be lost entirely. The white reversed variant keeps all five primitives legible at 16 px.
- Auto-switch example:
  ```html
  <link rel="icon" href="/brand/favicon.svg" type="image/svg+xml">
  <link rel="icon" href="/brand/favicon.ico" sizes="any">
  <link rel="icon" href="/brand/favicon-dark.ico" media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)">
  ```
